# Figure captions

Prefer short, dry captions that describe what is shown, with interpretation normally left to the main text.
Name the plotted quantities and system, then identify panels or panel groups in order, giving shared conditions once.
Include only the conditions and processing details needed to read the figure, such as temperature, composition, averaging, or normalization.
Explain markers, lines, shading, and error bars where their meaning is not already clear, and flag scale differences that affect comparisons.
Occasionally call out a specific observation or add a brief supported interpretation when it materially helps the reader understand the figure.
Match the manuscript's notation, panel-label style, and use of short descriptive caption titles, keeping simple captions to one or two sentences and expanding only as the figure requires.
Check panel references and factual details against the figure and available source material, and flag missing information rather than filling it in.
